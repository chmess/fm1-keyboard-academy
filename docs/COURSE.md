# Course design

Keyboard Academy is designed around short, repeatable learning loops rather
than a long scrolling song. Each activity introduces one idea, demonstrates it,
adds guided practice and then removes assistance.

## Learning loop

1. **Orient:** name the goal in one sentence.
2. **Model:** play the complete exercise once at its learning tempo.
3. **Guide:** wait for the learner and illuminate the expected key when help is enabled.
4. **Correct:** flash red after a wrong pitch but keep the musical position.
5. **Assess:** calculate pitch accuracy and a simple score.
6. **Adapt:** replay after inactivity, repeat below 80%, or move forward at mastery.

There is no fail state. Repetition gets slower and more explicit instead of
punishing the learner. Known melodies arrive only after keyboard geography,
five-finger movement and pulse have been introduced separately.

## Chapters

| Chapter | Lessons | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Find the Keys | 3 | Locate middle C and distinguish C, D and E. |
| Five Fingers | 3 | Establish a compact C-position and smooth direction changes. |
| Rhythm | 3 | Separate timing from pitch using repeated notes and rests. |
| Steps and Skips | 3 | Hear and play adjacent tones and thirds. |
| Easy Songs | 10 | Transfer the foundation to familiar melodies. |
| Folk Songs | 10 | Build phrase memory and longer musical arcs. |
| Classics | 5 | Introduce wider ranges and quicker note patterns. |
| Challenge | 5 | Perform complete melodies with reduced assistance. |

## Scoring

Pitch accuracy is `correct / (correct + mistakes)`. A correct note is worth 100
points and a wrong attempt removes 25 points without making the score negative.
The initial mastery threshold is 80%. `PERFECT`, `GREAT`, `GOOD` and
`KEEP GOING` are descriptive feedback, not grades.

## Content policy

Foundation exercises are original and intentionally short. Included melodies
are short traditional or public-domain teaching excerpts. Contributions should
avoid copyrighted contemporary melodies unless redistribution rights are clear.
